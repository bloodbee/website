When you think of online training, you inevitably think of all those charlatans in the e-commerce world who promise the moon and the stars. Their courses are *generally disappointing* and fail to improve your life.

This is the exact opposite of what Floriane Métou offers. As a physical therapist who has treated over 1,000 patients, she runs a training website focused on a few specific conditions chosen for the daily effort they require from patients.

The first course offered by [Physio Flo](https://physio-flo.com) focuses on fibromyalgia, a condition that causes muscle and joint pain, physical and mental fatigue, and poor-quality sleep. 

![Main page website physio flo](/media/physio-flo-homepage.png "Home page of the website")

This training is a **self-care program** that provides patients with the keys and practical tools they need to gradually regain their independence and more effectively manage this condition in their daily lives.

This program is available in two versions, both of which provide access to the [Teachable](https://teachable.com) learning platform:
- [autonomous](https://fibro-flo.teachable.com/l/pdp/formation-fibro) - a self-paced version, which includes the entire course
- [accompagnied](https://fibro-flo.teachable.com/l/pdp/formation-fibro-accompagnee) - a guided version, which also includes individual sessions with Floriane

There is also the option to join a WhatsApp support group, whose members all live with this disease.

![Physio flo program](/media/program.png "Self-care program")

Furthermore, Floriane plans to provide training courses on other diseases in the future.

For the stack, I used [VueJS](https://vuejs.org/) for the frontend, [TailwindCSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) for the design, [Netlify](https://netlify.com) as host provider and [OVH](https://www.ovhcloud.com/fr/) as domain provider.

*If you know someone living with fibromyalgia, tell them about this website—they'll be eternally grateful to you.*
